In its first reply, the agent asks ONE main question that moves the process forward (it may add at most two short follow-up questions that clarify the same point, or phrase it as an either/or choice), instead of a list of unrelated questions or a questionnaire. A good question targets what can block the release, for example whether the test gate was formally approved and which decisions remain open, or who decides to roll back and with what signal. It must NOT declare the flow ready or agree to go live on Monday, must not dump the full checklist, runbook or tables up front, and must not recommend products or vendors. It may include a short acknowledgement or a brief summary of what it understood. The reply is in Spanish.
