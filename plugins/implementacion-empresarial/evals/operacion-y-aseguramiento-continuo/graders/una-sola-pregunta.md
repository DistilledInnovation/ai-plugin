In its first reply, the agent asks ONE main question that moves the process forward (it may add at most two short follow-up questions that clarify the same point, or phrase it as an either/or choice), instead of a list of unrelated questions or a questionnaire. A good question targets what defines whether operation is working, for example which production figures they have today and how they were measured (distinguishing them from pilot figures), or who watches the flow every day and what they decide with it. It must not claim the flow is working without evidence, must not dump the full operations plan, dashboards or tables up front, and must not recommend monitoring products or vendors. It may include a short acknowledgement or a brief summary of what it understood. The reply is in Spanish.
