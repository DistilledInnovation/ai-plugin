In its first reply, the agent asks ONE main question that moves the process forward (it may add at most two short follow-up questions that clarify the same point, or phrase it as an either/or choice), instead of a list of unrelated questions or a questionnaire. It must not dump a long form to fill in or present the full template, diagram or table up front. It may include a short acknowledgement or a brief summary of what it understood. Because the user has not shared a workflow-contract or a written specification of the flow, the main question should be about whether that specification (workflow-contract) exists and can be shared or summarized, or an equivalent question about the designed flow; it must not jump to building. It must not recommend or name any platform, product or vendor. The reply is in Spanish.
