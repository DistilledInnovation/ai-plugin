In its first reply, the agent asks ONE main question that moves the process forward (it may add at most two short follow-up questions that clarify the same point, or phrase it as an either/or choice), instead of a list of unrelated questions or a questionnaire. A good question targets what decides how much can be guaranteed, for example whether the events system accepts an operation key or lets the flow check whether the registration already exists before writing, or what happens today when that step runs twice. It must not dump a long form to fill in or present the full template, register or tables up front, it must not promise that duplicates will never happen, and it must not recommend products or vendors. It may include a short acknowledgement or a brief summary of what it understood. The reply is in Spanish.
