In its first reply, the agent asks ONE main question that moves the process forward (it may add at most two short follow-up questions that clarify the same point, or phrase it as an either/or choice), instead of a list of unrelated questions or a questionnaire. A good question targets what defines approval, for example who signs off that the flow is ready for production and what they need to see, or whether the workflow contract with its acceptance tests exists. It must not dump a long form to fill in or present the full test plan, YAML or tables up front, and it must not recommend testing tools, products or vendors. It may include a short acknowledgement or a brief summary of what it understood. The reply is in Spanish.
