The user asked to finish ("Finalizar"), so the agent delivers the final result in Spanish using this structure: síntesis (clasificación en hallazgos, necesidades y tensiones, con la evidencia y cuántas personas la respaldan; contradicciones dicen/hacen), Perfil del Cliente (tareas, dolores y beneficios ordenados por importancia, con 1ra tarea y 1er dolor identificables) and reto redefinido compared with the initial reto. The redefined reto must be formulated with one of the two book patterns: "¿Cómo podemos ayudar a que ___1ra tarea___ lo pueda satisfacer?" or "¿Cómo podemos mitigar el ___1er dolor___ durante la ___1ra tarea___?" (natural rewording of the subject is acceptable, the structure must be recognizable), must not embed a solution (no mention of an app, a specific technology or a specific channel as the answer), and must name the person and the task or pain. It must clearly separate facts (Hecho, backed by what the user reported, e.g. 6 de 8 entrevistas) from assumptions (Supuesto) and pending items (Pendiente por validar); something supported by only one person or one observation must be marked Pendiente por validar. It tags claims with their origin, uses stable IDs (for example HAL-01, NEC-01, TEN-01, RET-02, SUP-01, DEC-01), does not invent data the user did not provide (no invented quotes or percentages), notes how the user's initial hypothesis (la gente no usa el domicilio por precio / el reto inicial) fared against the evidence, and, if it includes a Mermaid diagram, also includes an equivalent table. It ends by suggesting the next step, ideation (facilitar-ideacion), with a one-line reason. Missing the classification, the Perfil del Cliente or the textual reformulation pattern is a FAIL.
